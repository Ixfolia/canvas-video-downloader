Canvas Video Saver

Options. Everything is stored locally in your browser.

Site access

The extension needs permission to see network responses on websites so it can notice the video files your course pages load. It is not granted yet.

Which pages to watch

The check is made against the address of the page in the tab, not the video server, so embedded players (Canvas Studio, Kaltura, Panopto) on an allowed course page are still detected even though their video files come from other hosts.

Downloads

Files are saved to Downloads/CanvasVideos/ by default.

Diagnostics

If nothing shows up while a video plays, this shows what the extension actually sees. URLs are shown without their query strings (which often contain sign-in tokens).
